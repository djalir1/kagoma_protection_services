1. How the system fits together

There are three parts, all sharing one database (Supabase project Kagoma-site):

PartAddressWhat it is for
Public website/, /about, /services, /industries, /training, /contactPresents Kagoma, takes security enquiries, and lists the courses.
Kagoma Academy platform/academy/…Portals for students, professionals, employers, clients and workers; the course player; certificates; the staff office.
Admin dashboard/adminSign in as an administrator to view and edit every record in the database.

Every person has one account (email + password). What they can open depends on the account’s role: student, professional, employer, client, staff or admin.

3. Training & Development

The page at /training is the front door to the academy. It shows:

  • The catalogue: 20 courses in two tracks. There are 12 Professional courses (KG-101 to KG-112) and 8 Executive Protection volumes (EP-V1 to EP-V8). You can search them and filter by track. A ▲ marks courses with a practical component, which certified instructors assess in person.
  • Check a Kagoma certificate: anyone can type a certificate number and see whether it is valid, suspended, revoked or expired.
  • The platform: links to each portal.
  • In-house corporate training: asks organisations to get in touch through the Contact page.

Lessons are bilingual (English and Kinyarwanda). The learner switches language with the RW/EN button in the portal.

4. Student journey: from sign-up to certificate

Create account→Enrol→Study lessons→Module practice quizzes→Final exam (70%)→Vetting by Kagoma→Certificate fee→Certificate issued
  1. Create an account. Open Training & Development → Student Portal and register with your name, email, password, date of birth, phone, national ID and address. The account starts as pending vetting.
  2. Enrol and study. Choose a course and press Enrol. Study is open: anyone signed in can start learning straight away. Progress is saved lesson by lesson and shows as a percentage.
  3. Practice quizzes. Each module ends with a short quiz. These are ungraded and can be repeated as often as needed.
  4. Final exam. Each course has a final exam drawn from the module question banks. The pass mark is 70% and a learner has 3 attempts. The result appears immediately.
  5. Vetting. Before a certificate can be issued, Kagoma staff must approve the learner’s identity (Vetting in the academy console). Some courses also require another course first: KG-108 needs KG-109, KG-110 needs KG-108, and KG-112 needs KG-106. The minimum age is 18.
  6. Certificate fee. After passing, the learner pays the certificate fee: 25,000 RWF for professional courses and 15,000 RWF for EP volumes. They pay by MoMo (*182*8*1*061457*amount#, payee KAGOMA PROTECTIONS SERVICES LTD) or by bank transfer, then enter the transaction reference in the portal.
  7. Confirmation and issue. Staff confirm the payment under Payments. Once the full fee is confirmed, the certificate is issued automatically, as long as the exam is passed and, for ▲ courses, a practical pass is recorded.
If a course’s certificate fee is set to 0 (or left empty), the certificate is issued the moment the final exam is passed.

Applying through staff instead (optional)

A learner can also apply for a course. Applications appear under Applications in the academy console. When staff approve one, the system checks eligibility (vetting, age and prerequisites) and enrols the learner.

5. Certificates and verification

  • Each certificate has a number like KAGOMA-CERT-2026-000001 and a private verification token for its QR or link.
  • Statuses: valid, suspended or revoked. A certificate past its expiry date shows as expired.
  • Public check: the box on /training and the Certificate Register at /academy/certificates.html. They show only the holder’s name, course, issue date and status.
  • Only administrators can suspend or revoke a certificate, and every change is written to an audit log (Certificate Audit).
  • Holders see their own certificates in the Student Portal and can open a printable copy (/academy/certificate.html).

7. Work applications and event staffing

Apply for Work (talent pool)

Casual workers (stewards, ushers, accreditation staff and so on) register at /academy/work.html with their name, phone, ID, full Rwandan address (province, district, sector, cell, village), roles, experience and availability. Applicants must be 18 or older. Staff screen them as new → screened → approved / standby / rejected.

Events (Event Desk, /academy/staffing.html, staff only)

  1. Staff create an event (name, venue, date, call time, hourly rate, staff needed) on the Event Desk.
  2. To advertise it, set publish. It then appears as an open shift on the Apply for Work page, and pool members can offer their availability using their phone number.
  3. Staff add people to the roster from the pool, or share the event’s join link or QR code so workers can register themselves on the day.
  4. At the gate, staff clock people in and out. Hours and pay are calculated from the event’s rate, and staff can adjust a shift with a note.
  5. The manifest lists every worker’s ID and address for the authorities. Payroll totals people, shifts, hours and amount.

8. The admin dashboard (/admin)

  1. Go to /admin and sign in with an administrator email and password. Accounts without admin rights are refused.
  2. Overview shows key counts (people, enrolments, certificates, requests, payments, applications) and buttons to the academy console and staff office.
  3. The left sidebar lists every table, grouped as People, Training, Certificates, Clients & sales, Payments, Staffing & jobs, Office. The number beside each table is its record count. On a phone, use the drop-down at the top instead.
  4. Open a table to search it (text columns), page through it 25 records at a time, and use the buttons:
    • New: add a record. Leave a field blank to use its default (IDs, dates and reference numbers fill themselves in).
    • ✎ Edit: change a record. Only the fields you change are saved.
    • 🗑 Delete: remove a record after confirming. Records that depend on it, such as a module’s lessons, may be deleted with it.
  5. Use Sign out at the bottom of the sidebar when you finish.
Edit with care. The dashboard writes straight to the database. For day-to-day work such as approving people, confirming payments and issuing certificates, use the academy console (next section). It runs the business rules: eligibility checks, automatic certificates and audit logs.

Staff office (/academy/office.html)

The office creates company documents with automatic references: agreements (KPS-AGR), service orders, quotations (KPS-QTN), invoices (KPS-INV), receipts (KPS-RCT), NDAs, risk assessments, post orders, incident reports, service reports, employment letters, employee rules, the code of conduct and warnings. It also holds the company profile and the managing director’s signature, which only staff can see.

Event Desk (/academy/staffing.html)

The Event Desk is where staff run events: the roster, offers from the talent pool, clock-in and clock-out, shift adjustments, the manifest and payroll (see section 7). For client events it also shows whether the client is cleared: a signed agreement, an approved order and the deposit paid.

11. Payments

  • MoMo: merchant code 061457. The portal fills in the dial string *182*8*1*061457*<amount># for the learner.
  • Bank: Equity Bank Rwanda PLC, account 4002201404367, KAGOMA PROTECTIONS SERVICES LTD.
  • The learner enters the transaction reference from the SMS. Each reference can be used only once.
  • Staff confirm payments under Payments in the academy console. Confirming a certificate fee in full issues the certificate.
  • Online card and MoMo checkout (Flutterwave) is built in but switched off. It turns on when psp_live is set to true in Company Settings after the merchant account is approved. It also needs a server-side payment webhook, which is not set up yet.
  • Course study is currently free: course fees are empty. To charge for a course, set its fee_rwf.

12. Setup checklist and maintenance

  1. Create the first admin. In Supabase go to Authentication → Users → Add user, enter the admin email and password, and tick Auto Confirm User. Then make that account an admin. The last step in kagoma-website/sql/47_admin_dashboard.sql does this.
  2. Load the course content (modules, lessons, quizzes, final exams). In Supabase go to SQL Editor → New query, paste the course-content file and press Run. This is a one-off step. Afterwards the summary should list 20 courses, each with modules, lessons and final-exam questions.
  3. Email sign-up. Supabase sends a confirmation email to new accounts by default. Check Authentication → Email templates, and set the Site URL (Authentication → URL Configuration) to the live domain.
  4. Certificate emails. Certificates queue a message in Email Outbox. Sending those emails needs an email service to be connected, which is not set up yet.
  5. Backups. Supabase keeps daily backups on paid plans. Export important tables regularly on the free plan.

13. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to check
“Invalid login credentials” at /adminThe account must exist in Authentication → Users and be confirmed. Reset the password there if needed.
“Not an administrator”The account’s role in Profiles must be admin.
“This course has no content loaded yet”The course-content step in section 12 has not been run.
Certificate not issued after passingCheck vetting status, prerequisites, the certificate fee (confirmed in full?) and, for ▲ courses, a practical pass.
A professional cannot be listedThey need approved vetting and at least one valid certificate.
Contact form does not sendCheck the internet connection and the browser console. Requests appear in Service Requests.
