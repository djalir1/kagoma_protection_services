3. Why we use your data

  • To create and manage user accounts
  • To provide training and other Kagoma services
  • To verify identity and eligibility
  • To conduct lawful vetting and accreditation
  • To manage event personnel and assignments
  • To communicate with users, staff, clients and venues
  • To monitor course progress and assessments
  • To issue and verify certificates
  • To calculate working hours and applicable payments
  • To maintain operational, accounting and tax records
  • To protect the security of our systems, personnel and clients
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations
  • To respond to legitimate requests, complaints or disputes

Kagoma does not sell personal data to advertisers.

4. National ID and other identification information

Where National ID or other identification information is requested, Kagoma uses it only for a defined and legitimate purpose: identity verification, lawful vetting, accreditation, or a requirement imposed by a client, a venue or applicable law.

We do not use identification information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

5. Who may receive your data

Access is restricted to people or organisations that reasonably need it for a legitimate purpose.

Kagoma personnel. Authorised staff may access the information required for their assigned responsibilities. Access is granted according to role and withdrawn when it is no longer required.

Clients and venues. Where you are assigned to work at a client or venue and that organisation legitimately requires information for site or event accreditation, Kagoma provides the minimum required for that purpose: name, identification information, phone number and relevant address information.

The client or venue does not receive unrelated information such as your course results, account information or payment history, unless there is a separate lawful reason to provide it.

Certificate verification. Someone verifying a certificate receives only what is necessary to confirm it is genuine: the holder’s name, the course, the certificate information and the date of issue.

Verification does not disclose your email address, phone number, National ID, address, assessment marks or payment information.

6. Service providers and data processors

Kagoma uses trusted third-party providers — hosting, email, payment, authentication and technology services — to operate its services.

Where such providers process personal data on Kagoma’s behalf, Kagoma takes reasonable steps to ensure they are subject to appropriate confidentiality, security and data-protection obligations.

7. International transfers

Some technology or service providers used by Kagoma may process or store information outside Rwanda.

Where personal data is transferred outside Rwanda, Kagoma takes reasonable steps to ensure the transfer and processing comply with applicable Rwandan data-protection requirements, and that appropriate safeguards are applied where required.

8. Payment information

Kagoma may retain a payment reference or transaction confirmation where necessary to confirm a payment, issue a certificate, maintain accounting records or comply with applicable tax requirements.

Kagoma does not collect or store your card PIN, mobile-money PIN or any other payment authentication credential.

Where payment is processed by a third-party provider, that provider’s own terms and privacy practices may also apply.

9. How we protect your data

Kagoma uses reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorised access, unauthorised disclosure, loss, alteration, misuse and destruction.

Access to systems and records is limited according to a person’s role and operational need.

No internet-based system can be guaranteed completely secure. Kagoma takes reasonable measures to protect the information entrusted to it.

10. How long we keep your data

Kagoma keeps personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Account and course informationWhile the account remains active, and for any further period reasonably required for operational, legal or dispute-resolution purposes
Certificate recordsRetained longer, including after an account is deleted, because a certificate may need to remain verifiable for employment, professional or educational purposes
Event accreditation informationDeleted, returned or otherwise disposed of when no longer required, subject to legal or contractual requirements
Payment and accounting recordsFor the period required by applicable tax, accounting and financial-record obligations

11. Your rights

Subject to applicable law and its exceptions, you may:

  • Ask what personal data Kagoma holds about you
  • Ask for a copy of it
  • Ask for inaccurate or incomplete information to be corrected
  • Ask for deletion where the law allows
  • Object to certain processing
  • Ask for certain processing to be restricted
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Raise a complaint about how your personal data is handled

Write to info@kagomaprotection.com. Kagoma responds within the period required by law, and in any case within 30 days, subject to lawful extensions or exceptions.

12. Certificate records and account deletion

Deleting your Kagoma account does not necessarily mean every record connected with it can be deleted.

Kagoma may retain a limited certificate record where reasonably necessary to establish that a certificate was genuinely issued and to allow future verification. What is retained is limited to what that purpose requires.

You may also ask for your name to be withheld from any public or professional directory operated by Kagoma.

13. Cookies and similar technologies

The Kagoma website uses limited browser storage necessary to operate the service: whether you are signed in, your selected language, and your light or dark display preference.

Kagoma does not use this website for advertising-based behavioural tracking. If that changes, this notice will be updated and any required information or choices provided.

14. Children

Kagoma’s training and account services are intended for adults. Kagoma does not knowingly provide services requiring an adult account to anyone under 18.

If you believe Kagoma holds personal data relating to a person under 18 without an appropriate lawful basis, write to info@kagomaprotection.com.

15. Data breaches and security incidents

If Kagoma becomes aware of a personal-data security incident, it assesses the incident and takes appropriate measures in accordance with applicable law, including any required notification to the relevant authority or to affected individuals.

16. Changes to this Privacy Notice

Kagoma may update this notice to reflect changes in its services, technology, legal requirements or data-processing practices.

Where a change materially affects your rights or how your personal data is used, Kagoma provides appropriate notice before it takes effect where required. The latest version is published on this site.
